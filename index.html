Buenos Aires, Argentina

I'm 33 and have spent more than a decade in customer-facing roles — clothing stores, a restaurant floor, a hotel front desk. I studied Psychology for four years in San Luis, Argentina, and after moving to Italy — where I lived for four years — I pointed that same curiosity toward a different field: Big Data and data analysis. I also tried my hand at web development, but found I prefer researching, organizing information and supporting processes, leaving the more technical side of coding to others. Oh! Ink Studio — case studyOh! Ink Studio — caso de estudioOh! Ink Studio — caso di studio

While building this portfolio, I was also building my own shop. Oh! Ink Studio is my print-on-demand business on Etsy: I design every product in Kittl and Canva, send it to production through Printify, and handle everything else from there — optimizing each listing so it surfaces in the right searches, tracking which categories convert best, planning a launch calendar around the season, and responding to every customer the way I'd want to be responded to. It's still a small, growing shop, but it's also my lab: every SEO or pricing tweak is something I can apply directly to the business of whoever hires me.
